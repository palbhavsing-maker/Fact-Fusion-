FACT #01

🌍 Earth isn't a perfect sphere

Earth's rotation makes it slightly wider around the equator than from pole to pole.

FACT #02

🪐 Saturn is extremely low-density

Saturn's average density is lower than water. That's why scientists often use the famous "floating Saturn" comparison.

FACT #03

🦒 Giraffes have 7 neck vertebrae

A giraffe has seven cervical vertebrae — the same number humans have.

FACT #04

🌌 Space is incredibly vast

Our Solar System is only a tiny part of the enormous Milky Way galaxy.

FACT #05

⚡ Lightning is extremely hot

The air around a lightning channel can become much hotter than the surface of the Sun.
